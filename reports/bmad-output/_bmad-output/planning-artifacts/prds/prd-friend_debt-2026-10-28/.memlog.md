- (event) PRD run started as Create; no prior in-progress PRD found
- (event) User directed answers from docs/friend_debt_service.md; Zoho source story file was not in the repo
- (assumption) Problem: a creditor records a debt on someone who is already a friend; the debtor is notified in the financial app and pays through the host; the debt row is never deleted
- (assumption) Actors: verified-merchant or bank-customer creditor; bank-customer debtor; host app Banky or another embedder; system admin is operations with no v1 screen
- (assumption) v1 is record, send, accept or reject, single and group pay, forgive a partial, remind, dispute with documented resolution, and append-only history; package does not add friends, move money, verify merchants, or own accounts
- (assumption) Spec locks are product decisions unless the user reopens them: never delete, two creditor types, host moves money, active friendship required, eight statuses
- (assumption) No product success metrics in the spec; form factor is an embeddable module inside the host financial app
- (change) Technical mechanism from the spec (embedding, onPay, tenancy, record shape) captured in addendum.md, not the PRD
- (decision) Fast path. MVP has no revenue; success is loyalty to the Host app.
- (decision) A successful host payment is confirmed by backend-to-backend verification, so a dead client does not lose the payment or double-charge.
- (decision) Historical invoices classify each settled amount as paid cash, paid from other app, or waived. Waived is not collected cash.
- (decision) Friend management stays in the host. The invoice service is its own tenant and only views friends. The friend API comes later; v1 uses a mock.
- (assumption) Paid cash is recorded by the creating creditor and does not call onPay. Paid from other app means a verified host payment.
- (assumption) System admin gets one Unpaid debts screen inside the module. Dispute resolution is one note and one outcome, with no ticket thread.
- (assumption) Tone is a calm bank record: Arabic status labels, waived labeled as waived, no collections voice. Loyalty window assumed as 30 days with no numeric target.
- (event) Fast-path draft written to prd.md for review. Not finalized.
- (decision) User struck loyalty window and success rate. Section 12 is deferred. No SM, no 30-day window, no numeric target. Supersedes the earlier loyalty-as-success decision.
- (decision) User confirmed Paid cash as a creditor-recorded action with no host payment, and confirmed the Unpaid debts screen for system admin inside the module. Host marks the operations session.
- (decision) Invoice label paid is a host payment after backend verification. Invoice label paid from other app is a payment from an app other than the host. Supersedes the assumption that paid from other app meant the host.
- (decision) Dispute resolution is one note and one outcome. No thread, assignee, or timer.
- (assumption) Other-app payment is recorded by the creating creditor, same bounds as paid cash, and does not call onPay. The user defined the label, not a second payment rail.
- (decision) Real customers come from the friend import. Huda, Sami, and Nabil are scene labels only. This service stores a nickname and can change it without updating the friend import.
- (assumption) A nickname is private to the signed-in user. The other person keeps a separate nickname. Empty nickname shows the imported name.
- (decision) Nickname is the signed-in user's private label on his own friend list. The other person does not see it. It does not change the friend import or the creditor name on a notification.
- (decision) Voice confirmed: short bank record, Arabic status labels, a waived amount is called waived even when status is Paid.
- (decision) Paid from other app is recorded by the creditor the same way as cash. Another app does not push that payment.
- (decision) Every in-app notification shows its notification date. The due date stays off the notice. On the send notice the notification date is the registration date.
- (decision) An edit to invoice-line description, amount, or quantity is allowed and is the only dispute. It is not payable until the other partner approves it, rejects it, or the approval wait ends. Default wait is 5 days, or a period the backend specifies, then the edit auto-approves.
- (decision) A host payment for a total posts only onto still-open debts. It excludes a debt that is disputed or that received cash or a waiver while the transfer was in flight. Extra host money is not recorded as cash, waiver, or a payment on a disputed debt.
- (decision) After unfriend, create and send stop. The debtor can still pay and approve or reject an edit. The creditor can still record cash, record an other-app payment, waive, remind, and propose an edit.
- (decision) While an edit waits, the debtor can still pay the approved outstanding in part or in full. The proposed lines are not payable. The screen may show many lines or many debts, and that action is one payment to the host app.
- (decision) User confirmed: while an edit waits, the debtor pays the approved outstanding in part or in full; proposed lines are not payable; many lines or debts show on screen and the host receives one payment; a full payment of the approved amount drops the waiting edit.
- (event) PRD finalized
